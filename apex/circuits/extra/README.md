The historical F1 outlines are by Jules Roy / F1 Circuits SVG, distributed through [F1DB](https://github.com/f1db/f1db/tree/main/src/assets/circuits/black-outline) under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-F1-CIRCUITS.txt). APEX samples and rescales these outlines at load time.

The Le Mans outline is derived from the [Track Atlas 24h midline and start/finish marker](https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/8c12ac414ea74ffe4a3ef74b294a2eafab7ee60d/tracks/circuit-de-la-sarthe/raw/layers/24h.geojson) by Tobi, under the [MIT license](LICENSE-TRACK-ATLAS.txt). The geographic coordinates have been projected to a local SVG plane.

The circuit outlines provide horizontal geometry. Pit facilities, runoff and other generated features are provisional APEX geometry.

Nordschleife and the T13 pit lane use [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/41395670) raceway geometry © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The SVG contains the joined one-way raceway segments and T13 pit lane projected to local coordinates. The timing line is placed at T13 and the lap is rescaled to the [official 20.832 km length](https://www.nuerburgring.de/info/nuerburgring/records?locale=en).
